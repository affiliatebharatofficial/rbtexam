### PRACTICE QUESTIONS

#### Question 1 (Domain A: Measurement | Difficulty: Easy)
An RBT records the exact time that elapses between the delivery of the instruction "Please open your workbook" and the moment the learner begins turning to the assigned page. What measurement procedure is being utilized?
- A. Duration
- B. Inter-Response Time (IRT)
- C. Latency
- D. Momentary Time Sampling

#### Question 2 (Domain A: Measurement | Difficulty: Medium)
During a 30-minute circle time session, an RBT sets a timer for 3-minute intervals. The RBT looks up at the precise moment the timer sounds and records whether the client is sitting in their assigned seat. What discontinuous measurement method is this?
- A. Whole-Interval Recording
- B. Partial-Interval Recording
- C. Momentary Time Sampling
- D. Permanent Product Recording

#### Question 3 (Domain A: Measurement | Difficulty: Medium)
A supervisor asks an RBT to track how much time elapses between consecutive occurrences of vocal stereotypy (from the end of one episode to the start of the next). Which metric should the RBT record?
- A. Rate
- B. Inter-Response Time (IRT)
- C. Latency
- D. Frequency

#### Question 4 (Domain B: Assessment | Difficulty: Easy)
An RBT places five preferred toys in front of a child in a straight line. The child selects one toy and is allowed to play with it for 30 seconds. The RBT removes the selected toy from the array, rearranges the remaining four toys, and instructs the child to select another. Which preference assessment is being conducted?
- A. Paired Stimulus Assessment
- B. Multiple Stimulus With Replacement (MSW)
- C. Multiple Stimulus Without Replacement (MSWO)
- D. Free Operant Preference Assessment

#### Question 5 (Domain B: Assessment | Difficulty: Hard)
While conducting a functional assessment observation, a client engages in aggression. The RBT notes: Antecedent = Math worksheet placed on desk; Behavior = Client hits RBT's arm; Consequence = RBT removes worksheet and gives a 2-minute break. What type of assessment data did the RBT collect?
- A. Direct ABC Narrative / Descriptive Data
- B. Functional Analysis Experimental Probe
- C. Indirect Rating Scale
- D. Preference Hierarchy Assessment

#### Question 6 (Domain C: Skill Acquisition | Difficulty: Medium)
An RBT teaches a child to wash hands using a 7-step task analysis. In the initial phase, the RBT physically prompts steps 1 through 6, and teaches the child to independently complete step 7 (drying hands on a paper towel) to access reinforcement. What chaining procedure is being implemented?

- D. Behavior Shaping

#### Question 7 (Domain C: Skill Acquisition | Difficulty: Easy)

#### Question 8 (Domain C: Skill Acquisition | Difficulty: Hard)

### ANSWER KEY & EXPLANATIONS

| Q# | Answer | Explanation & Clinical Rationale | Exam Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **C** | **Latency** measures the temporal locus—the exact time between the presentation of an antecedent stimulus ($S^D$) and the initiation of the response. Duration measures total time from start to finish. | Look for words like "between instruction and initiation" $\rightarrow$ Latency. |
| **2** | **C** | **Momentary Time Sampling (MTS)** records whether the behavior is occurring at the precise end of an interval. Whole-interval requires the behavior throughout the entire duration; Partial-interval requires at least once anytime during the interval. | MTS saves time because the observer only looks up when the timer rings. |
| **3** | **B** | **Inter-Response Time (IRT)** is the elapsed time between two consecutive occurrences of the same response (end of instance 1 to start of instance 2). | IRT is inversely related to rate (shorter IRT = higher rate). |
| **4** | **C** | In **Multiple Stimulus Without Replacement (MSWO)**, selected items are removed from the array on subsequent trials, generating a ranked hierarchy. In MSW, the chosen item is returned. | "Without Replacement" means array shrinks by 1 item each trial. |
| **5** | **A** | Recording Antecedent, Behavior, and Consequence in real-time as they naturally occur is **Direct ABC Narrative/Descriptive Data**. Functional Analysis involves experimental manipulation. | ABC data identifies potential environmental correlations in natural settings. |
| **6** | **B** | **Backward Chaining** teaches the final step of the chain first, providing immediate access to the natural terminal reinforcer upon completion of the last step. | Backward chaining ensures the client always experiences the finished product. |
| **7** | **B** | Pointing, nodding, or gesturing toward the correct stimulus is a **Gestural Prompt**. Positional prompts involve moving items physically closer. | Gestural prompts guide without physical touch. |
| **8** | **C** | Standard DTT protocols require a structured **Error Correction procedure** (e.g., $S^D \rightarrow$ immediate prompt $\rightarrow$ transfer trial $\rightarrow$ distractor trial) to prevent learning errors. | Never reinforce an error or display negative emotion during DTT. |
| **9** | **A** | **Stimulus Generalization** occurs when a single trained response occurs across novel antecedents, settings, people, or stimuli. | Same behavior across different settings = Stimulus Generalization. |
| **10** | **B** | Tokens, money, and praise gain their reinforcing properties through pairing with primary (unconditioned) reinforcers like food or water. | Tokens are always conditioned/secondary reinforcers. |
| **11** | **B** | **Extinction** involves discontinuing reinforcement for a previously reinforced behavior. If attention maintained screaming, withholding all attention implements extinction. | Extinction requires identifying and withholding the maintaining function. |
| **12** | **C** | **DRO (Differential Reinforcement of Other Behavior)** delivers reinforcement contingent on the complete absence (zero occurrence) of the target problem behavior during a specified interval. | DRO = "Zero behavior required during interval." |
| **13** | **A** | **DRI (Differential Reinforcement of Incompatible Behavior)** reinforces a topographically incompatible behavior that cannot be performed simultaneously with the target behavior. | Sitting on floor is physically incompatible with standing up. |
| **14** | **B** | An **Extinction Burst** is an immediate, temporary increase in the frequency, intensity, and variability of a behavior when reinforcement is first withheld. | Warn parents about extinction bursts so they do not give in. |
| **15** | **B** | An **Establishing Operation (EO)** is a motivating operation that increases the current value of a reinforcer (e.g., water deprivation increases the value of water). | EO increases value; AO decreases value. |
| **16** | **C** | Behavior-analytic documentation must be **objective, observable, and measurable**. Words like "furious," "sad," or "stubborn" describe internal unobservable states. | Avoid mentalistic words (angry, stubborn, sad) in session notes. |
| **17** | **B** | RBTs are **Mandated Reporters**. Suspected physical, emotional, or sexual abuse or neglect must be reported immediately to child protective services and the BCBA. | Mandated reporting cannot be delayed or delegated away. |
| **18** | **C** | BACB ethical standards and HIPAA require clinical documentation and raw data to be securely archived for at least **7 years**. | Remember the 7-year record retention rule for BACB compliance. |
| **19** | **B** | BACB Ethics Code for RBTs Section 1.07 prohibits accepting gifts with monetary value to prevent conflicts of interest and maintain professional boundaries. | Small hand-made client drawings are acceptable; gift cards/money are never allowed. |
| **20** | **B** | RBTs must receive ongoing supervision for a minimum of **5% of their total direct behavior-analytic service hours** each calendar month. | 5% monthly supervision is mandatory to maintain RBT credential. |
| **21** | **B** | RBTs must operate strictly within their **Scope of Practice**. Designing or modifying Behavior Intervention Plans is the exclusive responsibility of the BCBA. | Direct all treatment modification requests to the supervising BCBA. |
| **22** | **B** | A **Multiple (Dual) Relationship** occurs when a practitioner maintains a clinical relationship and an outside personal, social, or financial relationship with a client/family. | Dual relationships impair clinical objectivity. |
| **23** | **A** | **Most-to-Least Prompting** begins with the highest level of assistance (full physical) and systematically fades to lesser prompts (partial, gestural, independent). | Errorless learning typically uses Most-to-Least prompting. |
| **24** | **C** | The four functions of behavior are: Attention, Escape/Avoidance, Access to Tangibles, and Automatic/Sensory. "Revenge" or "Retribution" are non-behavioral constructs. | Mnemonic: E-A-T-S (Escape, Attention, Tangible, Sensory). |
| **25** | **B** | In a **Free Operant Preference Assessment**, the client has unrestricted access to all items in an environment, and engagement time with each item is recorded. | Free Operant = No demands, continuous open access. |

### FLASHCARD OPPORTUNITIES (Article 1)
1. **Front**: What is Latency in ABA measurement?
   - **Back**: The elapsed time between the onset of an antecedent stimulus ($S^D$) and the initiation of the learner's response.
   - **Memory Tip**: "L" for Latency = "L"ag time before starting.
2. **Front**: What is Inter-Response Time (IRT)?
   - **Back**: The elapsed time between two consecutive occurrences of the exact same target behavior.
   - **Memory Tip**: IRT is the time *between* repetitions.
3. **Front**: What is Momentary Time Sampling (MTS)?
   - **Back**: Recording whether the behavior is occurring at the exact moment an interval ends.
   - **Memory Tip**: MTS = "At this exact moment."
4. **Front**: What is an MSWO Preference Assessment?
   - **Back**: Multiple Stimulus Without Replacement: The selected item is removed from subsequent arrays to establish a hierarchy.
   - **Memory Tip**: Array shrinks by 1 item each round.
5. **Front**: What is DRO?
   - **Back**: Differential Reinforcement of Other Behavior: Delivering reinforcers contingent on zero occurrences of problem behavior during an interval.
   - **Memory Tip**: DRO = 0 (zero) behavior required.
